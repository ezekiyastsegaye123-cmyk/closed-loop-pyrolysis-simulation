The Problem in Conventional Pyrolysis

Traditional fast pyrolysis heats dry biomass (wood, crops) to ~500 °C without oxygen to generate liquid biofuel. However, standard systems suffer from two fatal weaknesses:

  • Massive External Fuel Waste: Continuous external natural gas or electric heating is required, severely degrading carbon savings and raising operating costs.
  • Low-Quality Bio-Oil: Raw crude bio-oil contains corrosive organic acids, heavy polycyclic tars, and reactive oxygen that causes polymer aging and phase separation.
The Closed-Loop Breakthrough

By reintegrating all process byproducts internally, the plant operates with complete self-sufficiency:

  • Zero External Heating (Autothermal): Solid ash carrier reheated to 800 °C recirculates into the reactor, producing +4,390 MJ/h energy surplus and reducing auxiliary fuel to 0.0%.
  • Char-Catalyzed Tar Cracking: Byproduct biochar loaded with inherent minerals (AAEMs) cracks heavy tars into light distillates.
  • Syngas Hydrodeoxygenation (HDO): Recycled H₂/CO syngas deoxygenates vapors in situ, producing stabilized, drop-in bio-oil.

Comprehensive Materials & Chemical Streams Guide

In this closed-loop biorefinery, no material is wasted. Every feedstock input, solid carrier, vapor fraction, and byproduct gas is engineered to serve an exact thermodynamic or chemical catalytic purpose:

RAW INPUT FEEDSTOCK
Baseline: 1,000 kg/h dry basis · T: 25 °C Ambient

1. Biomass Feedstock (Wood Residue & Agricultural Biomass)

Composition: High-molecular-weight renewable biopolymers composed of Cellulose (~45%), Hemicellulose (~30%), and Lignin (~25%), with moisture content dried below 10 wt% and ground to particle sizes $d_p \le 2$ mm.

Chemical & Thermodynamic Purpose: Biomass is the primary carbon-neutral source of hydrocarbons. When injected into the oxygen-free 500 °C reactor, it undergoes rapid primary thermal cracking under Semyonov kinetic principles:
  • Cellulose & Hemicellulose rapidly crack into light volatile oxygenates (forming bio-oil precursors) and non-condensable CO/CO₂ gases.
  • Lignin decomposes across a wider temperature range into phenolic oligomers, heavy aromatic tars, and solid biochar.
CIRCULATING HEAT CARRIER
5,940 kg / 1,000 kg feed · T: 800 °C (Hot) / 500 °C (Spent)

2. Recirculating Solid Ash Heat Carrier

Composition: Inert solid inorganic mineral ash and refractory silica carrier with high specific heat capacity ($C_p = 950 \text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$).

Chemical & Thermodynamic Purpose: The heart of autothermal operation! Instead of burning fossil fuels through metal reactor walls (which causes extreme fouling and low heat transfer), hot ash is heated to 800 °C in the combustor and delivered directly into the reactor bed.
  • Delivers 1.69 GJ/h of direct sensible heat: $Q_{\text{delivered}} = \dot{m}_{ash} C_{p,ash} (800 - 500) \text{ °C}$.
  • Provides instantaneous conductive heat transfer ($>1000 \text{ °C/s}$) directly to biomass particles, maximizing bio-oil liquid yield.
  • Returns via the lower transfer chute at 500 °C to be reheated by byproduct combustion, forming a continuous closed thermal loop.
SOLID BYPRODUCT & CATALYST
Yield: 22.0% (220 kg/h) · LHV: 22.0 MJ/kg · Active AAEMs

3. Biochar (Solid Carbon & In-Situ Cracking Catalyst)

Composition: High-carbon porous solid matrix containing concentrated alkali and alkaline earth metals (AAEMs: Potassium, Calcium, Magnesium, Sodium) inherently present in plant ash.

Chemical & Thermodynamic Purpose: Serves two high-value closed-loop roles:
  • Combustor Reheating Fuel (70%): 154 kg/h of char is combusted in the 800 °C vessel, releasing 3,388 MJ/h of zero-cost thermal energy to reheat the circulating ash.
  • In-Situ Catalytic Tar Cracker (30%): Separated by the primary cyclone and layered in the secondary bed, the hot char's porous surface and AAEMs catalytically break down heavy polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons into light condensable fuels, lowering oil viscosity and stopping pipe fouling without needing expensive commercial catalysts.
REACTIVE ATMOSPHERE & FUEL
Recycle Ratio: 0.50 (50%) · LHV: 12.0 MJ/kg · H₂ / CO Rich

4. Non-Condensable Syngas (H₂ / CO Recirculation)

Composition: Permanent gases including Hydrogen ($H_2$), Carbon Monoxide ($CO$), Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$), and light alkanes ($CH_4$).

Chemical & Thermodynamic Purpose:
  • Hydrodeoxygenation (HDO) Reactive Atmosphere (50% recycled): Pumped into the bottom plenum of the reactor, the $H_2$ and $CO$ create a reducing environment. Active hydrogen species strip reactive oxygen atoms from volatile pyrolysis fragments as water ($H_2O$) and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$). This deoxygenation dramatically improves bio-oil stability, prevents phase aging, and elevates its heating value.
  • Combustor Energy Supplemental (50% excess): Excess syngas is routed to the combustor burners to provide clean, high-temperature heat alongside biochar.
PRIMARY LIQUID PRODUCT
Yield: 23.5% (235 kg/h) · HHV: ~28 MJ/kg · Stabilized

5. Upgraded Bio-Oil (High-Calorific Liquid Biofuel)

Composition: Deoxygenated liquid hydrocarbons, light aromatics, and stabilized phenolic derivatives with significantly reduced moisture and acid content.

Chemical & Commercial Purpose: This is the primary commercial product of the plant. Unlike crude bio-oil which degrades within weeks, this upgraded bio-oil is chemically stabilized by in-situ HDO and char catalysis:
  • High Higher Heating Value (HHV ~28 MJ/kg vs crude 16-19 MJ/kg).
  • Low acidity ($pH > 4.5$), low viscosity, and no phase separation during long-term storage.
  • Ready as a drop-in maritime biofuel, industrial heating fuel, or co-refining feedstock for Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF).
COLD-START SYSTEM ONLY
Steady State: 0.0% Throttle · LHV: 46.1 MJ/kg

6. Auxiliary Start-up Fuel (LPG / Trim Fuel)

Composition: Liquefied Petroleum Gas ($C_3H_8 / C_4H_{10}$) with high energy density (46.1 MJ/kg).

Operational Purpose: Auxiliary fuel is strictly required during the initial cold-start phase to preheat the cold ash carrier and refractory liners up to 800 °C. Once the plant produces its own biochar and syngas, the intelligent Fuzzy Logic + PID supervisory controller automatically throttles the LPG burner down to 0.0%. In normal operation, zero fossil fuel is consumed!

Interactive Simulator Guide & Hands-On Experiments

Use the real-time controls skid on the right side of the screen to interact with the computational solver. Here are four recommended experiments to explore the physical laws governing closed-loop pyrolysis:

EXPERIMENT 1

Verify Zero-Fuel Autothermal Operation

Action: Check the top KPI dock and the control skid.

What to look for: Notice that Aux Start-up Fuel is 0.0% while the Autothermal Balance shows +4,390 MJ/h. The energy from combusting 70% of the byproduct char and excess syngas is more than enough to maintain the 800 °C solid carrier loop, proving self-sustaining autothermal operation!

EXPERIMENT 2

Disable Char Catalytic Cracking

Action: Toggle the "Char Catalytic Cracking" switch OFF in the right controls panel.

What to look for: Watch the Product Distribution Yields bars update. Without AAEMs active mineral catalysis in the char bed, heavy polycyclic aromatics cannot be cracked, causing bio-oil recovery to drop and heavy tars to accumulate.

EXPERIMENT 3

Scale Feedstock Throughput

Action: Drag the Biomass Feed Basis slider from 1,000 kg/h up to 2,000 kg/h.

What to look for: Observe how the Ash Heat Carrier Flow immediately increases from 5,940 kg/h to nearly 11,880 kg/h to satisfy the higher endothermic enthalpy demand ($\Delta H = +1.0 \text{ MJ/kg}$). The 3D particle streams also accelerate dynamically to represent higher mass flow!

EXPERIMENT 4

Test Manual Burner Override

Action: Switch ON the "Manual Override Control" toggle.

What to look for: The automatic Fuzzy Logic + PID supervisory controller is disabled, revealing the Manual LPG Trim Burner slider. Drag the slider to 50% and watch the temperature graph spike, or drop it to 0% and test how long the solid ash carrier keeps the reactor steady!
